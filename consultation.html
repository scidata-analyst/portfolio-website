Questions

Got Doubts?

01

Is the consultation really free?

100% free. No credit card, no obligation. We genuinely enjoy talking about technology and helping people think through their projects — even if we're not the right fit.

02

Who will I be speaking with?

You'll speak directly with our senior team — either our founder or a lead strategist. No salespeople, no account managers. Just people who build things every day.

03

What should I prepare?

Just come with your ideas and questions. If you have any reference sites, sketches, or documents, bring those too — but they're not required. We'll guide the conversation.

04

How is this different from just emailing you?

A live conversation lets us ask the right questions, explore tangents, and get to the heart of your challenge in minutes — something that takes days over email. Plus, you'll see our faces and know who you're working with.

05

What if I'm not ready to start a project yet?

Perfectly fine. Many of our consultation calls are with people who are still in the thinking phase. We'll help you clarify your ideas and figure out the next steps — no pressure to commit.
